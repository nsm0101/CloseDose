# Sick Newborn: First 15 Minutes clinical specification

## Intended population

Ill-appearing infants from birth through 28 days presenting to a provider who
may not routinely stabilize neonates.

## Clinical purpose

Organize parallel first actions and consultation triggers across hypoglycemia,
sepsis, congenital heart disease, adrenal crisis or CAH, metabolic disease, and
possible maltreatment without claiming to diagnose the infant.

## Evidence baseline

No single source set has been approved. Neonatal resuscitation, neonatal sepsis,
hypoglycemia, critical congenital heart disease, endocrine, metabolic, and child
protection guidance must be selected, dated, and reconciled before development.

## Explicit non-goals

- No diagnosis ranking, exclusion rule, sepsis calculator, medication dose,
  fluid directive, feeding recommendation, or disposition decision.
- No replacement for neonatal resuscitation training or immediate consultation.
- No identifiers, birth narrative, or persistent case record.

## Questions requiring clinical resolution

- Which actions are universal and which depend on gestational age or delivery
  context?
- Which glucose, oxygenation, temperature, perfusion, and laboratory thresholds
  are source-specific and safe to display?
- How are ductal-dependent disease, CAH, metabolic decompensation, HSV, and
  maltreatment prompts kept visible without diagnostic overreach?

## Required named reviewers

Neonatology, PEM, pediatric pharmacy, pediatric cardiology, endocrinology,
metabolic genetics, infectious diseases, child protection, emergency nursing,
institutional policy, and regulatory review.

## Simulation acceptance cases

- A hypothermic lethargic neonate surfaces warming, glucose assessment, airway
  support, vascular access preparation, and consultation in parallel.
- A cyanotic neonate does not encourage blind saturation normalization.
- A vomiting dehydrated neonate keeps endocrine and metabolic consultation
  triggers visible.
- Unknown gestational age produces uncertainty prompts rather than assumptions.

## Release boundary

Remain Planned until the evidence set and every threshold are approved, all
dose-containing content has pharmacy review, and community-ED simulations pass.
