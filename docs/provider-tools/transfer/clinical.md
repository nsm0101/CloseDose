# Peds Transfer Ready clinical specification

## Intended population

Children and adolescents being stabilized for interfacility transfer from a
community, rural, or mixed-age emergency department in the United States.

## Clinical purpose

Organize work into stabilize now, call now, before departure, transport-team
requirements, family preparation, records and images, and structured verbal
handoff. The application supports process reliability and does not select a
destination, accepting clinician, transport mode, or treatment.

## Evidence baseline

- EMSC Pediatric Interfacility Transfer Guide, 2026.
- Local transfer agreements, transport-team requirements, EMTALA process, and
  institutional escalation policy are mandatory configuration inputs.

## Explicit non-goals

- No diagnosis, disposition, treatment, medication, ventilator, or transport
  level recommendation.
- No guarantee that a child is stable for departure.
- No patient identifiers, accepting-facility identifiers, or stored transfer
  record.

## Questions requiring clinical resolution

- Which instability findings must keep a stabilization action pinned first?
- Which equipment and personnel prompts vary by transport capability?
- How are critical images, cultures, blood products, medication infusions, and
  pending results represented without implying completion?
- Which family, consent, language-access, and safeguarding prompts are required?

## Required named reviewers

PEM, pediatric critical-care transport, community or rural EM, emergency
nursing, pediatric pharmacy for medication-preparation prompts, institutional
transfer leadership, legal/compliance, and regulatory review.

## Simulation acceptance cases

- A rural ED can find the receiving-center call and airway stabilization lists
  within three interactions and 20 seconds.
- A ventilated child case separates bedside stabilization from transport-team
  equipment and personnel checks.
- A pending-culture case produces an identifier-free handoff that marks the
  result pending rather than completed.
- An incomplete checklist never displays ready for departure.

## Release boundary

Remain Planned until source mapping, institutional configuration, named review,
community-ED simulation, privacy review, and regulatory assessment are complete.
