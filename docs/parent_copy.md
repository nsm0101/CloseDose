# Parent-first copy guide

## Goals
- Use calm, short sentences.
- Avoid medical jargon when possible.
- State the action clearly.

## Good vs. not good

| Not good | Better |
| --- | --- |
| “Administer antipyretic per kilogram dosing.” | “Give the dose based on your child’s weight.” |
| “Contraindicated for infants under 6 months.” | “Not recommended for babies under 6 months.” |
| “Medication schedule exceeded.” | “It’s too soon for another dose.” |
| “Seek emergent care immediately.” | “Call 911 for emergencies.” |

## Microcopy examples
- “Nothing logs automatically. Tap confirm when you are ready.”
- “Night Zoom: make the dose easy to read.”
- “If you’re worried, it’s okay to get help.”
