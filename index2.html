Experimental lab

Parent-first accessibility + dose timeline (seamless-but-worse prototype)

This page intentionally stays low-fi and a little “worse” while still fitting in the layout so we can spot friction fast. Use it to stress-test caregiving flows before we polish the UI.

Seamlessly worse, on purpose: rough copy + loud hierarchy + oversized prompts so we can discover what parents actually need in the moment.

Accessibility boosters for parents & caregivers

  • Calm Mode: ultra-high contrast, larger type, and “one-tap” zoom for night feeds.
  • Hands-free dosing: voice entry + spoken confirmation (“You are giving 5 mL now”).
  • Caregiver handoff: share a read-only “dosing card” with grandparents + babysitters.
  • Neurodiversity support: dyslexia-friendly fonts, reduced motion, and icon-first guidance.
  • Multi-language quick switch: tap-to-translate all labels, doses, and warnings.
  • Emergency clarity: always-visible “when to seek care” banner in the timeline view.

Dose tracking + scheduling time course with alerts

  • Timeline strip: last dose, next safe window, and max-per-day limits in one row.
  • Smart alerts: gentle reminders, escalating to backup caregivers after missed doses.
  • Context-aware warnings: alert if meds overlap or if temperature/weight changed.
  • “Stuck” rescue: quick button for vomiting/spit-up to log and adjust the plan.
  • Quiet hours: configurable “do-not-disturb” with emergency override.
  • Offline safety: cache last-approved regimen + dosing card if the device is offline.

Account integration & connected care

  • Account sync: caregivers, pediatrician, and school nurse roles with permissions.
  • Pharmacy bridge: refill reminders, concentration changes, and lot/bottle tracking.
  • EHR + portal: import weight, allergies, and med lists to reduce manual entry.
  • Wearables: watch alerts + “confirm dose” from a lock screen.
  • Secure sharing: temporary access links with auto-expiration.

Disclaimer

This calculator is for educational support only and does not replace guidance from your pediatrician or pharmacist. Always confirm dosing before administering medication.

CloseDose was created with the intention of providing dosing information of common over-the-counter medications for generally healthy children.

If your child is 0-2 months of age, has any complex past medical history or past surgical history, any previous reactions to administered medications, history of allergic reaction, or has significant personal or family history of liver/kidney disease please consult with your medical care team prior to use of CloseDose pediatric medication dosing calculator.

When to seek care: Call your pediatrician or seek urgent care for trouble breathing, persistent vomiting, signs of dehydration, seizures, or fever lasting more than 3 days.
