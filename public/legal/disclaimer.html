Infant Breathing Guide

The Infant Breathing Guide uses representative animations to help caregivers describe visible breathing patterns. It cannot diagnose or rule out illness, examine a child, measure oxygen, hear breath sounds, or determine that a baby is safe. Real signs can look different from the examples and may occur in different combinations. Absence of a depicted sign must not delay urgent or emergency care when a baby appears unwell or a caregiver is concerned.

Emergency & Return Precautions

If you suspect an overdose, severe reaction, or your child appears very ill, call your local emergency number or poison control immediately. In the United States, call 911 for an emergency. Always follow medication labels and instructions from your clinician.
