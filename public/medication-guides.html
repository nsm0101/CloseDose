Safety tips

  • Do not exceed 5 doses in 24 hours.
  • Under 6 months: may dose every 4–6 hours as needed.
  • Over 6 months: dose every 6 hours as needed.
  • Alternating acetaminophen and ibuprofen every 3 hours (offset by 3 h) can improve fever and pain control — ask your pediatrician.
  • All liquids on this page share the same 160 mg / 5 mL concentration — they are interchangeable.
  • Keep a log of medication, time, and dose to prevent accidental repeat dosing.
  • Seek care for any infant under 2 months with fever, or for fever lasting more than 3 days in older children.

Safety tips

  • Do not give to infants under 6 months unless directed by a physician.
  • Allow at least 6 hours between doses. Do not exceed 4 doses in 24 hours.
  • Give with food or milk to reduce stomach upset.
  • Infant drops (50 mg / 1.25 mL) and Children's suspension (100 mg / 5 mL) require different dosing charts — never use one chart for the other product.
  • Avoid in children with kidney disease, dehydration, or known NSAIDs allergy.
  • Alternating with acetaminophen every 3 hours can improve pain and fever control — confirm the schedule with your pediatrician first.

Medication Safety & Usage

This tool does not provide medical advice. The dosing information is for educational purposes only and should be verified with a healthcare provider or pharmacist before use.

  • Always use the measuring device (oral syringe or dosing cup) that came with the medication — household spoons are not accurate.
  • Confirm your child's current weight if it has been more than 3 months since the last measurement.
  • Contact Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) immediately if an overdose is suspected.
