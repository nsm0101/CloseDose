EnFit vs Legacy Connector
ISO 80369-3 Standard
≈

EnFit (Current Standard)

Purple or teal ribbed external threads
8 mm diameter, asymmetric thread pitch
Physically incompatible with IV Luer-lock

Legacy (Being Phased Out)

Smooth cone tip, clear or white plastic
Luer-lock compatible (the hazard it creates)
Requires adapter to use with EnFit tubing
Safety Note

EnFit was specifically designed to prevent wrong-route errors — an IV syringe cannot physically connect to an EnFit port. If legacy connectors are still in use, alert the care team to initiate transition per facility protocol.
