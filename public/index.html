How to use CloseDose

  1. 1Pick an age group. Tap the band that matches your child.
  2. 2Enter the weight. Pounds or kilograms — both work.
  3. 3Calculate. Get a single recommended dose with the safest interval.

Safety quick-reference

  • Always use the syringe or cup that came with the medicine.
  • Re-check your child's weight every few months.
  • Wait at least 6 hours between doses of the same medicine.
  • Ibuprofen is not for infants under 6 months.

When to call for help

Call 911 for trouble breathing, persistent vomiting, or seizures.

Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222

Any fever in a child under 2 months is a medical emergency — contact your pediatrician immediately.
