When to seek care

  • Trouble breathing, blue lips, or severe wheezing.
  • Very sleepy, hard to wake, or not making eye contact.
  • Signs of dehydration: no tears, dry mouth, or no wet diapers.
  • Seizure, severe pain, or fever lasting more than 3 days.
  • Call 911 for emergencies. If you’re worried, it’s okay to get help.
