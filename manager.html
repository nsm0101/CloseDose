Patient Manager

Track active patients during shift—rooms, plans, and dispo at a glance.

Touchscreen: long-press a card to open or collapse for editing, double-tap to add a timer, tap status pills to toggle.
Desktop: click “Edit info” or right-click a card to edit, use the bed selector in the header row, and scroll/drag to navigate.
